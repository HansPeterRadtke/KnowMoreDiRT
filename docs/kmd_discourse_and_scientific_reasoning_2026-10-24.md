## Result 1: Does DRS improve contextual retrieval?

**NOT established for the actual KMD parser or corpus.** DRS formalism can encode discourse reference, scoped negation, accessible subordinate boxes, conditional antecedents, temporal distinctions and identity links, but correct encoding depends on a fallible extractor. A simple parent/neighbor retrieval baseline with source identity and scope tags can recover many of the same distinctions. The DRS-specific marginal value remains unmeasured in real input. Formalism alone is not an outcome.

Local reproducible *synthetic mechanism probe*: `python3 research/devtests_drt_reference/scoped_evidence_probe_20261024.py` runs twelve fixed hand-authored examples without a model. Exact-chunk-only: **four of twelve** correct, errors on dream, fiction, quote, negation, conditional, expiration, unauthorized speaker, conflict. Neighbor-text heuristic: **eleven of twelve** correct; fails the unauthorized speaker. Correctly supplied structured scope/source/time metadata: **twelve of twelve** correct. The third is explicitly an **oracle upper bound**, NOT a measured DRS extraction result and NOT an independent heldout generalization test. The comparison only establishes that making scope available to decisions can fix these controlled cases; it does not establish DRS adds value beyond provenance-rich neighbor retrieval or that KMD will extract those scopes.

## Result 2: Can system derive, contradict and falsify?

**Sound conditional derivation is possible from explicitly represented premises; reliable autonomous new scientific conclusions and real-world falsification are NOT established.** A rule such as P and (P implies Q) entails Q in the chosen logic, conditional on premise meaning and truth. Two opposite claims conflict only when proposition, referent, world, tense, applicable conditions and authority align; existence of conflicting reports does not identify which one is false. Lack of evidence for P does not establish not-P in an open-world corpus. A hypothesis is empirically falsified only by a discriminating, valid observation that the hypothesis excludes, under explicit assumptions and uncertainty. A DRS is not by itself an experimental test or premise verification mechanism. The accompanying probe JSON includes two **illustrative, declared** examples of derivation and conflict; these are not evidence of an implemented autonomous KMD discovery/falsification engine.

### Actual newly frozen source-backed discourse experiment

The earlier twelve-case hand-authored synthetic scope probe remains a mechanism illustration, **not a DRS result**. The new fixed evaluation excerpts are drawn from **ten exact char-offset ranges** in original, authentic KMD/agent voice-recording transcripts under the Infra original-recording tree. The unedited excerpts, original relative source identifiers, source file SHA-256, character offsets, target span, and manual pre-run gold are in `research/devtests_drt_reference/heldout_recordings_scope_gold_20261024.json`. The gold was frozen before model inference; the source builder now refuses conflicting overwrites. The scope categories are asserted, dream, hypothetical example, conditional, proposal, and speculative. “Asserted” means claimed by the speaker, not independently true. These are real recordings rather than newly written synthetic narratives, but the small set was **manually selected and gold-labeled by one analyst**; no independent second human or unseen post-design selection verifies external generalization.

One non-model source-plus-context keyword baseline (`heldout_scope_compare_20261024.py` with no `--model`) classified **6/10** correctly. The same excerpts were sent with a **single frozen prompt, no few-shot tuning, temperature zero and 56-token budget** to the existing Raspi CPU `TinyLlama-1.1B OpenOrca Q4_0` GGUF via llama-cpp-python 0.3.9, with context 1024, two CPU threads and no shared GPU. Its simplified DRS-like `world` and `modality` frame yielded **1/10 correct**, **4/10 malformed/invalid structures**, in **35.1 seconds**, with approximately **1.2 GiB peak process RSS**. Full per-case outputs, including wrong repeated `world=dream` classifications and raw invalid frames, are in `heldout_recordings_model_vs_neighbor_20261024_results.json`. The small model often emitted the same dream/conditional frame for unrelated assertions and philosophical discussions. Zero schema-constrained recovery retries were performed. This is negative evidence for **this small model/prompt/extraction interface**, **not** an evaluation of full production KMD DRS JSON, **not** a Qwen benchmark, and **not** a fair claim that provenance will always beat DRS. In particular, the gold selection and heuristic baseline were not double-blind, and the baseline had access to a complete source excerpt. These figures therefore remain exploratory, not independently replicated heldout production-quality estimates.

A distinct *post-hoc exploratory* eighty-character chunk split and literal token-overlap retrieval experiment used the same frozen excerpts. Anchor recall after two neighbor chunks on each side was **10/10** in the *already selected relevant document excerpt*; raw single-chunk scope classification was **5/10**, neighbor-expanded **6/10** (`heldout_chunk_retrieval_20261024.py`). This shows recoverable local context matters, but is **not** realistic corpus-scale retrieval or an independent test set. The model saw complete excerpts and did not actually use the deployed KMD parser or query executor. It would be scientifically invalid to call these measured numbers evidence that formal DRS is inherently worse or inherently better.

### New independent SAT counterexamples and corrected narrow research inference

The earlier **13/13** propositional and **7/7** toy observational cases still pass; this does not establish general correctness. A new fixed-seed **512-case** scoped Boolean implication stress test (`scoped_reasoning_property_audit_20261024.py`) compared the previous research-only forward chaser against an **independent SymPy satisfiability oracle**. It scored **509/512**: the three errors were unsupported `unknown` responses where a **negative** consequence was logically implied (modus tollens/contrapositive chains), not false claims of successful entailment. One explicit counterexample is the jointly scoped premises `not d`, `b implies d`, `b implies c`, `c implies d`; the independent oracle refutes `c`, but the earlier forward-only algorithm returned unknown. The original test results and source-premise identifiers are preserved unmodified.

An isolated, non-production research implementation now performs **both forward implication and justified backward propagation from explicit negated consequents**, while retaining source indices and blocking dream/quotation and mismatched-date evidence from current-world proofs. It matches independent SymPy on **512/512 previously inspected cases** and on **1,024/1,024 new untouched fixed-seed propositional cases**. For comparison, the earlier forward-only method scored **1,007/1,024** on those new cases, missing seventeen valid refutations. Eight new unittest functions also pass, including an additional sixty-four independently scored seeded trials, dream and time isolation, same-scope contradictions, explicit source-linked proof paths, open-world unknown gating and no inferred referent identity. This is reproducible evidence for **valid narrow conditional conclusions and source-linked contradiction detection under stipulated premises**; it does not claim general first-order reasoning, identity/coreference extraction, real-world premise verification, new experimental discoveries or autonomous scientific falsification.

A falsification check is valid only when the tested hypothesis entails a prediction and actual verified observations conflict with that prediction under matching identity, scope, date and measurement conditions. The earlier seven stipulated observational cases still demonstrate this narrow contract, including rejection of merely missing evidence or scope-mismatched counterexamples; **no empirical study independently establishing a novel real-world falsification was run**. Website frequency and correlated model answers were never treated as proof.

**Answer one:** scope preservation is demonstrably necessary and a cheap provenance/neighborhood baseline handles many cases; whether validated model-extracted DRS improves real answer accuracy, false-fact prevention, attribution and cross-file identity over a fully optimized provenance baseline remains **unanswered** without a safe intended-model slot and independent narrative gold. The local TinyLlama failures are evidence for an inadequate local extraction setup, not a formal refutation of DRT/DRS.

**Answer two:** source-preserving formal systems **can** prove new *conditional consequences*, show which scoped premises are inconsistent, and reject stipulated hypotheses through independently valid counterexamples. This pass both identified a missing negative-inference rule in the first prototype and verified a bounded repair against a separate SAT implementation on held-out formal cases. **Automatic discovery of true new empirical facts, sound extraction from arbitrary natural language and real-world hypothesis falsification remain unverified**. The project still requires an honest model-backed end-to-end test that separates premise-extraction accuracy, logical validity, provenance, contradiction reports and independently confirmed external observations.

No production KMD APIs, original gold expectations, Infra guidelines, large model servers or user histories were modified. This is an isolated research result awaiting review, not a completed production feature or universal DRS recommendation.
