## Result 1: Does DRS improve contextual retrieval?

**NOT established for the actual KMD parser or corpus.** DRS formalism can encode discourse reference, scoped negation, accessible subordinate boxes, conditional antecedents, temporal distinctions and identity links, but correct encoding depends on a fallible extractor. A simple parent/neighbor retrieval baseline with source identity and scope tags can recover many of the same distinctions. The DRS-specific marginal value remains unmeasured in real input. Formalism alone is not an outcome.

Local reproducible *synthetic mechanism probe*: `python3 research/devtests_drt_reference/scoped_evidence_probe_20261024.py` runs twelve fixed hand-authored examples without a model. Exact-chunk-only: **four of twelve** correct, errors on dream, fiction, quote, negation, conditional, expiration, unauthorized speaker, conflict. Neighbor-text heuristic: **eleven of twelve** correct; fails the unauthorized speaker. Correctly supplied structured scope/source/time metadata: **twelve of twelve** correct. The third is explicitly an **oracle upper bound**, NOT a measured DRS extraction result and NOT an independent heldout generalization test. The comparison only establishes that making scope available to decisions can fix these controlled cases; it does not establish DRS adds value beyond provenance-rich neighbor retrieval or that KMD will extract those scopes.

## Result 2: Can system derive, contradict and falsify?

**Sound conditional derivation is possible from explicitly represented premises; reliable autonomous new scientific conclusions and real-world falsification are NOT established.** A rule such as P and (P implies Q) entails Q in the chosen logic, conditional on premise meaning and truth. Two opposite claims conflict only when proposition, referent, world, tense, applicable conditions and authority align; existence of conflicting reports does not identify which one is false. Lack of evidence for P does not establish not-P in an open-world corpus. A hypothesis is empirically falsified only by a discriminating, valid observation that the hypothesis excludes, under explicit assumptions and uncertainty. A DRS is not by itself an experimental test or premise verification mechanism. The accompanying probe JSON includes two **illustrative, declared** examples of derivation and conflict; these are not evidence of an implemented autonomous KMD discovery/falsification engine.

## Published neural DRS against independent PMB test gold — continuation October 24, 2026

The earlier twelve synthetic probes are not evidence of a measured model DRS benefit. This separate external experiment directly inspected **real neural DRS parser output**, already published by Rik van Noord's [Neural_DRS](https://github.com/RikVN/Neural_DRS) at immutable commit `81bcb26dfcc08f0da999cc7beaa2952f8820860c`, and **898 human-corrected original Parallel Meaning Bank 3.0 test DRSs and source sentences** from the authors' [DRS_parsing](https://github.com/RikVN/DRS_parsing) commit `190838f9adea2af616eb76156d2e66c8565a6c6e`. The exact upstream external corpora remain read-only under `/data/tmp/kmd-pmb-{reference,eval}-20261024`; no licenses were presumed that would justify republishing full original test text in this repository. The original published parser's `bert_char_1enc/output1.txt` predicts source-utterance DRS clauses and was not regenerated with the KMD model. The authors' original `eval.txt` separately reports 0.8897 Counter clause F1 for that individual parse and mean 0.89204 over five runs. A local original Counter command ran successfully; its lengthy detailed tail did not preserve a separate numeric local aggregate, so the repository's published score remains clearly **source-reported**.

Two new *read-only*, source-hash-checked analyses of the original parser output, **not gold-prefilled representations**, compare whether the parser correctly generates specific scope operators against the human-corrected gold, versus deliberately simple untrained lexical cue rules on those **same** 898 natural sentences: `research/devtests_drt_reference/pmb_external_20261024/pmb_semantic_scope_compare.py` and `pmb_operator_ablation.py`, with complete operator-wise confusion counts and source examples in adjacent JSON. **Negation-presence (not exact target scope):** 79 of 898 gold instances had a negation box. Actual generated DRS output detected 79/79, with 1 false positive: precision 98.75%, recall 100%, F1 99.37%; raw lexical markers detected 73/79 with 3 false positives: F1 94.19%. The lexical misses include real phrases like “anything but tidy,” “too tired to study,” and “instead of sugar”. An implicit-negation semantic analysis demonstrably recovers some distinctions beyond word matching.

In the **exploratory post-hoc** six-operator presence analysis, neural DRS vs basic lexical cues respectively scored: CONDITION F1 0.9508 vs 0.0606 (31 gold sentences), CONSEQUENCE 0.8772 vs 0 (31), POSSIBILITY 0.9714 vs 0 (18), ATTRIBUTION 0.8889 vs 0.3636 (18), and `YearOfCentury` 0.9091 vs **0.9412** (18; literal year regex did better). These are *not head-to-head architecture rankings*. In PMB, CONDITION includes implicit quantifier/modal restriction, not just the literal word “if”; the lexical cue baseline is consequently much weaker than a carefully scoped provenance/neighbor model. Presupposition/context and coreference clause links were **not** checked for correctness by operator presence alone. The parser still produced structural semantic errors despite good category detection; detecting a NEGATION box does not prove the **correct proposition** was negated. The test is short-utterance annotated semantics, not real cross-document identity, dreams, authored quotations, scientific facts or falsification.

**Measured conclusion:** a published neural DRS parser can automatically recover certain genuine implicit semantic phenomena more reliably than naive lexical matching, but its outputs are fallible and this benchmark does **not establish incremental end-to-end value over a source/provenance/neighbor retriever** on the owner's hard KMD tasks. Production `chunk-drs-v5` plus full KMD question retrieval has not yet been validated on an independent matching-budget narrative research corpus. No user gold fixture or former actor/identifier failures were modified.


## Source-grounded compact boxes versus direct local Qwen — October 24 controlled pilot

A stronger local model, isolated `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF` pinned revision `91cad51170dc346986eccefdc2dd33a9da36ead9`, Q4_K_M, was tested on **eight original externally expert-labeled SciFact paper–claim pairs**, four SUPPORT and four CONTRADICT, previously unused in this chat's model trials. The human label was used only to stratify selection **before freezing input**. Model prompts never saw label, annotated rationale sentence IDs or paper-answer gold. All source documents, actual text offsets, titles, full prompt, model-generated output, token costs and hashes are retained in `research/devtests_drt_reference/qwen_scifact_20261024/`; original external sources remain untouched. After all eight predictions, the original expert sentence IDs confirmed that **seven of eight had their actual annotated gold evidence included in the automatically selected source context** and, critically, **all four CONTRADICT cases included an expert annotated contradiction sentence**. These cases challenge model reasoning even with the relevant source in front of it.

Method A directly assessed whether the claim follows from the source+title. **Eight of eight verdicts were SUPPORT**, although the external human annotations classified half CONTRADICT, yielding **4/8** agreement. All four contradiction failures are concrete source conflicts: negative vs positive birth-weight/cancer association, **$5 billion** vs **$750 million** projected kidney donation savings, equal HIV progression rates vs **slower** rates with a stable partnership, and mice unable to generate neutralizing antibodies vs a mouse vaccine eliciting them. The model actually copied the contradicton-bearing source phrase for each case but still said SUPPORT. Only **7/8** copied evidence strings were exact source substrings; a copied phrase by itself is not sound entailment. Direct verdict cost approximately **189.8 CPU seconds** across eight cases.

Method B used the **same automatic source text** but required a source-only model extraction of a **compact DRS-inspired `boxes`/`conditions`/`polarity`/`evidence_quote` structure**, to be validated before a *second* verdict call. **All eight source-only extractions reached their fixed 195-token limit and produced incomplete JSON**, thus **zero valid grounded frames**. The semantic acceptance validator rejected them and safely returned UNKNOWN without invoking the second LLM judgment; approximately **380.6 additional CPU seconds** were consumed. The boxed route's “0/8 correct” should be read as **eight deliberate abstentions due to failed source parsing**, not eight measured incorrect deductions by a working DRT solver. The initial score file's `invalid_boxed=0` counts only the syntactically legitimate fallback UNKNOWN *verdict*, not the malformed extracted structures; the immutable followup source audit unambiguously counts eight failed/truncated boxes. There was **no silent repair, oracle substitution, larger-budget retry, shared inference server, or editing of gold**.

This CPU pilot is *NOT the intended KMD `chunk-drs-v5` production parser* and is **not** a fair matched-token architecture comparison: both methods saw identical source evidence, but the direct mode used one call and the boxed mode would require two calls with a larger semantic representation. A larger model or different budget may overcome truncation. Its robust contribution is *negative empirical grounding evidence*: a small real model made systematic polarity/number/scope mistakes in real scientific sentences despite original human evidence present, and a semantically richer representation was unreliable to produce. Alongside the published neural PMB scope extraction gains above, these findings illustrate the two distinct questions: (1) **can a parser extract scope with ground-truth accuracy?** and (2) **does that representation improve end-to-end grounded reasoning over a strong source+neighbor comparator?** Question (1) has positive evidence in controlled sentence-level neural PMB and negative evidence in this boxed local parser. Question (2), along with cross-document identity/dream/quote/time and genuinely original science, **remains unverified for KMD**. Preserve historic actor-identifier errors `hrq086`, `hrq087`, `hrq089` and both subsequent contextual negative cases; this work does not change source code or acceptance gold.

## Third continuation: independent ContractNLI source scope and neighboring evidence, 2026-10-24

To test beyond toy source stories and externally generated PMB *single* sentence structures, the original [ContractNLI corpus](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/contract-nli/) (Koreeda and Manning, *Findings of EMNLP* 2021, [doi:10.18653/v1/2021.findings-emnlp.164](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2021.findings-emnlp.164)) was obtained with original **CC BY 4.0** license and immutable source hash. It consists of real NDA texts with independently human-annotated `Entailment`/`Contradiction`/`NotMentioned` answers and exact evidence spans, including negation by exceptions, time/termination and party-specific scope. The complete unmodified dataset stays in Raspi research-only external `/data/tmp`; all local derived scripts, source digests and results belong to `research/devtests_drt_reference/contract_scope_20261024/`. **These legally attributed document judgments are not scientific truth, cross-file identity chains, dream/fiction scope or a real-world original discovery.**

The test contains **123 distinct public contracts × 17 claims = 2,091 human-labeled cases**: 968 entailment, 220 contradiction and 903 not mentioned. Original document and hypothesis is supplied as an *oracle source-document* retrieval setup, not full-corpus search. The independent untuned lexical Jaccard span ranking found at least one original expert-evidence span within its **top three** document spans for **435/968 entailments** and **59/220 contradictions**. Source-neighbor expansion by one annotated span on either side increased this to **590/968 entailments** and **109/220 contradictions**. This is a **measured, non-trivial gain in evidence visibility**, but contradiction evidence remains unusually hard to retrieve and a longer context can be more expensive or distracting. The 903 `NotMentioned` claims have no gold evidence, so span recall alone cannot measure their correct uncertainty. Exact source ranking and expert gold comparisons are persisted in `full_test_lexical_neighborhood_20261024.json`. Neither manually supplied DRS tags nor model votes are used to declare retrieved evidence true.

### Completed Qwen source-scope ablation on six separately authored real contracts

The frozen six-case real original ContractNLI test set produced **three different human meanings of the *same NDA-20 statement*** depending on which contractual source governs it. ## Overnight 2 continuation: actual DRS scope-content and referent correctness, October 25

An independently source-hash-checked follow-up to the real published neural PMB DRS comparison is documented in [`kmd_drs_scope_content_validation_2026-10-25.md`](kmd_drs_scope_content_validation_2026-10-25.md). Its key correction is that **operator-presence accuracy greatly overstates semantic faithfulness**. The trained parser detects the gold negation operator in **79/79** original 898 test sentences, but its subordinate boxes contain exactly the gold negated **predicate lemmas** in only **65/79**, and original word senses in only **54/79**. For **31** human-annotated condition→consequence sentence pairs, the parser emits both operator boxes in **25/31**, yet correctly encodes **both sides' predicate lemmas in 19/31** and lemmas+senses in **16/31**. Attribution operator presence is **16/18**, but the attributed predicate set is lemma-correct in **8/18** and sense-correct in **5/18**. A separate published 885-sentence dev split preserves similar qualitative distinctions without duplicating these precise proportions. There were also incorrect proper-name event roles: in the original sentence about a rock not hitting Mary, the gold puts Mary as the negated hit's **Destination**, whereas the model assigns her **Agent**; in another sentence it omits Tom from a neither-Tom-nor-Mary negation. Proper named negative event/role/referent content exactly matches gold **12/19** on the small original test subset. Full original model outputs and human gold were unmodified; all derived semantic metrics have source digests and counterexample trails. These are meaningful real parser errors that could corrupt logically sound deductions if accepted as axioms. **No full KMD production DRS vs provenance/neighbor end-to-end improvement is established**, and this linguistic semantic gold is not proof of independently new scientific discovery. Existing original hard actor-ID failures remain preserved.

### Overnight 3 continuation, October 25: source-only linguistic alternatives and actual v5 model validation
