## Result 1: Does DRS improve contextual retrieval?

**NOT established for the actual KMD parser or corpus.** DRS formalism can encode discourse reference, scoped negation, accessible subordinate boxes, conditional antecedents, temporal distinctions and identity links, but correct encoding depends on a fallible extractor. A simple parent/neighbor retrieval baseline with source identity and scope tags can recover many of the same distinctions. The DRS-specific marginal value remains unmeasured in real input. Formalism alone is not an outcome.

Local reproducible *synthetic mechanism probe*: `python3 research/devtests_drt_reference/scoped_evidence_probe_20261024.py` runs twelve fixed hand-authored examples without a model. Exact-chunk-only: **four of twelve** correct, errors on dream, fiction, quote, negation, conditional, expiration, unauthorized speaker, conflict. Neighbor-text heuristic: **eleven of twelve** correct; fails the unauthorized speaker. Correctly supplied structured scope/source/time metadata: **twelve of twelve** correct. The third is explicitly an **oracle upper bound**, NOT a measured DRS extraction result and NOT an independent heldout generalization test. The comparison only establishes that making scope available to decisions can fix these controlled cases; it does not establish DRS adds value beyond provenance-rich neighbor retrieval or that KMD will extract those scopes.

## Result 2: Can system derive, contradict and falsify?

**Sound conditional derivation is possible from explicitly represented premises; reliable autonomous new scientific conclusions and real-world falsification are NOT established.** A rule such as P and (P implies Q) entails Q in the chosen logic, conditional on premise meaning and truth. Two opposite claims conflict only when proposition, referent, world, tense, applicable conditions and authority align; existence of conflicting reports does not identify which one is false. Lack of evidence for P does not establish not-P in an open-world corpus. A hypothesis is empirically falsified only by a discriminating, valid observation that the hypothesis excludes, under explicit assumptions and uncertainty. A DRS is not by itself an experimental test or premise verification mechanism. The accompanying probe JSON includes two **illustrative, declared** examples of derivation and conflict; these are not evidence of an implemented autonomous KMD discovery/falsification engine.
