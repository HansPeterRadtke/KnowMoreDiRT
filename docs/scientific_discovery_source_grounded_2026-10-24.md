# Can KnowMoreDiRT support genuine scientific discovery? Corrective research, 2026-10-24

**Direct research answer.** Yes, computer systems have independently demonstrated genuine hypothesis generation, deductively valid mathematics and experimentally tested novel scientific conclusions, but their successful examples rely on something outside an LLM's opinion: an executable evaluator, independently reproduced laboratory observations or a carefully defined causal/experimental design. No published scientific result in the sources below proves that the current KnowMoreDiRT (KMD) system reliably generates *true, new discoveries* from arbitrary documents. The strongest supported option for the actual requested goal is a **source-preserving, scope-aware retrieval and evidence pipeline plus formal verification where premises support it and independently discriminating experiments for empirical hypotheses**. DRT/DRS is *one possible interpretation and long-distance reference layer*, not proof of truth and not a predetermined winner over a provenance-plus-neighbor baseline.

## Original research requirement, not an invented how-to question

Full original sources reread this pass: `../../infra/docs/guidlines/original-recordings/agents/Recording 2026-10-17 23-00-29-387.txt` and `Recording 2026-10-18 18-38-21-960.txt`, plus the three `original-recordings/kmd/` transcripts dated August fifth and sixth (actual source root `/data/infra/docs/guidlines`). Relevant complete normalized primary guidance is `knowledge_retrieval.md`, `kmd/knowledge_and_memory_design.md`, `agents/research_and_evidence.md`, and the research appendix, all under Infra guidelines. References here are original source-relative identifiers, **not accessible relative links from this separate repository**. The recordings' question is not simply whether an agent can summarize and agree with itself. It is whether a source-grounded research agent can distinguish genuinely unknown claims from negations, form new useful hypotheses, derive conditional consequences, seek contradictions, and have later independent outcomes decisively discriminate between them. Sources from separate authors, contexts and dates may disagree without logically contradicting one another; all statements must retain speaker, scope, time and epistemic status. The owner explicitly treats model ensemble votes as additional observations, never proof.

The proper evaluation outcome has several mutually exclusive evidence statuses:

- **Retrieved statement:** a quotation or document says a proposition; the system has not endorsed it as true.
- **Interpreted proposition:** a fallible extraction identifies referent, predicate, negation, attribution, time and conditional/dream/quotation scope; errors here contaminate all later proofs.
- **Derivation:** a rule yields a conclusion valid *if* its exact premises and scope are correct; a formal prover may check this without making the premises factual.
- **Contradiction:** scoped premises assert logically incompatible propositions in the same identity/conditions/time. This is evidence of **source disagreement or internal inconsistency**, not a decision about which source is true.
- **Research hypothesis:** an unobserved candidate explanation, mechanistic link or novel prediction potentially worth testing, often from non-obvious cross-paper connections.
- **Empirically tested outcome:** a measured intervention or discriminating observation bears on that prediction under declared assumptions; limitations, confounding, calibration and replication remain.
- **Scientifically established novelty:** independently adjudicated prior-art search and independent evaluation show the prediction or result adds something not previously known. “Novel to our database” is insufficient.

## Scientific primary evidence that directly answers the goal

**Robot Scientist Adam—novel biological conclusions and independent experimental checks.** King, Rowland, Oliver et al., *Science* 2009, “The automation of science” (doi: [10.1126/science.1165620](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1165620)), report an agent that constructed hypotheses about unknown yeast enzyme/gene functions and experimentally tested them through laboratory automation. The authors' follow-on detailed evaluation “Towards Robot Scientists for autonomous scientific discovery,” *Automated Experimentation* 2010 ([doi:10.1186/1759-4499-2-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/1759-4499-2-1)), says twenty hypotheses were proposed, twelve had strong robotic experimental confirmation, **three were additionally verified by conventional manual biological experiments** and **six more had support already discoverable by detailed literature searches**. A separate first-person retrospective in *Scientific American* reports seven conclusions as already known, one wrong and twelve novel candidates, illustrating a different denominator and classification; do **not** pool those numbers uncritically. This is exceptionally relevant negative evidence: a robot can generate valid, sometimes novel insight, but it can also call something novel when the literature already knew it, and it can make a wrong conclusion. Primary sources: [King 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19342587/), [2010 project follow-up](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1759-4499-2-1), and [authors' retrospective](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/rise-of-the-robo-scientists/).

**FunSearch—new formally checkable mathematical results, not an LLM vote.** Romera-Paredes et al., *Nature* 2024, [doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6). A language model generated executable programs, an external deterministic evaluator rated results, and evolutionary search retained candidates that improved on established constructions for cap sets and bin-packing heuristics. Its strongest claim is grounded by numerical/mathematical verification of a generated program. It does not show that a text-only agent may endorse new physical facts without measurements. General mechanism: **generative conjecture + independent objective test + counterexamples + preserved search provenance**.

**Coscientist—LLMs can link instructions, literature, instruments and experiments, with autonomy limits.** Boiko, MacKnight, Kline and Gomes, *Nature* 2023, [doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06792-0](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06792-0). Published tests include documentation-driven instrument operation, synthesis planning and palladium-catalysed cross-coupling reaction optimization with laboratory automation; the authors explicitly describe *(semi-)autonomous* experimental execution. This is evidence of useful workflow orchestration and physical feedback, not of universally reliable original mechanistic theories or removal of human laboratory boundaries.

**Literature-based discovery: Swanson's fish oil–Raynaud's connection.** D. R. Swanson, “Fish oil, Raynaud's syndrome, and undiscovered public knowledge,” *Perspectives in Biology and Medicine* 1986, [PMID 3797213](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3797213/). He joined disjoint research on fish oil/blood properties and Raynaud's vascular effects into a **hypothesis**, not a valid deductive certainty that fish oil treats the syndrome. The causal link requires biological assumptions and actual clinical verification. A subsequent independent randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial by DiGiacomo, Kremer and Shah, *American Journal of Medicine* 1989, [PMID 2536517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2536517/), randomized thirty-two primary/secondary Raynaud's patients. It found some positive measures for **primary** Raynaud's and no established improvement for **secondary** Raynaud's. It does **not** prove the original broad therapeutic hypothesis universally, and small samples require replication. This is a precise example of **literature-guided genuinely new conjecture followed by experimental constraints and subgroup-dependent conclusions**, without conflating corroboration with universal truth.

**Automatic equation/model discovery and causal invariance have different requirements.** Brunton, Proctor and Kutz, “Discovering governing equations from data by sparse identification of nonlinear dynamical systems,” *PNAS* 2016, [doi:10.1073/pnas.1517384113](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1517384113): sparse regression over observed dynamics can recover useful equations **given measured state variables, derivative estimation and candidate function libraries**; simulations and external predictive validation matter. Peters, Bühlmann and Meinshausen, “Causal inference by using invariant prediction,” *JRSS B* 2016, [primary manuscript](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~peterbu/Files/Manuscripts/invariant-causal-prediction.pdf): observational correlation alone is not a causal intervention; candidates remaining invariant across changed environments can support identification under stated assumptions. These methods are better for physics/causal claims when time series or interventions exist than purely textual DRS or model agreement.

**Document grounding and logical representations solve other, earlier failure modes.** Wadden et al., *EMNLP* 2020, [SciFact](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/), supplies published expert claim-to-paper evidence/rationale labels rather than assistant agreement. Jiang et al., *EMNLP Findings* 2020, [HoVer](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.309/), demonstrates that requiring support across two to four separate documents degrades semantic-matching accuracy. W3C [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) represents attributed entities, generated derivations, quotations, revisions and involved agents; it does **not** establish that a quoted claim is true. Formal DRT (Kamp, van Genabith and Reyle 2011; exact primary PDF in existing `docs/papers/`), OWL 2 [primer](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-primer/) and solver logic [SMT-LIB](https://smt-lib.org/) each provide different mechanisms: natural-language referents and subordinate scope; declarative ontologies with open-world interpretations; and satisfiability/entailment under supplied predicates, respectively. Probabilistic weights or source frequency do not automatically transform observation into truth. Source-dependent premises require source-dependent proof trees and trusted measurement protocols.

### Cross-publisher and historical claim scope: OPERA vs ICARUS

The scientific case was constructed from preserved primary archived source HTML, **not** hand-invented measurement numbers. The initial `real_neutrino_source_reasoning.py` automatically reads (a) the [historical CERN OPERA statement with its embedded dated 2012 updates](https://home.cern/opera-experiment-reports-anomaly-in-flight-time-of-neutrinos-from-cern-to-gran-sasso/), (b) OPERA's later timing instrumentation paper [arXiv:1212.1276](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1276) and (c) independent ICARUS's [arXiv:1208.2629](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.2629). **A real extraction failure was discovered:** arXiv's generic first page heading (“High Energy Physics - Experiment”) was mistakenly read as its paper title, and the older `real_neutrino_source_analysis_results_20261024.json` therefore mislabeled an **OPERA** measurement as **ICARUS**. The original failed output remains **unaltered** with a recorded SHA-256. The separately versioned `real_neutrino_source_reasoning_corrected.py` now reads the source's actual `citation_title`, `citation_author` and `citation_date` metadata, independently checks that paper/source identity matches, and produces `real_neutrino_source_analysis_corrected_20261024.json`. The corrected output identifies OPERA Collaboration, paper dated December 6, 2012, and ICARUS (Antonello et al.), paper dated August 13, 2012, as **different producer groups**; measured values remained unchanged. Exact source HTML SHA-256 values, source paragraph hashes, publisher, effective date, extracted numeric strings and original article metadata are retained. The original HTML is preserved read-only in `/data/tmp/kmd-scientific-realworld-sources-20261024`, not copied into Git. This negative result establishes that even an automatically correct numeric extraction can be scientifically invalid if the experimental actor is bound incorrectly. Do not silently relabel a previously failed result as a pass.

CERN's 23 September 2011 original release reported an **apparent** speed excess of approximately **twenty parts per million** over a **730-kilometre** neutrino flight. It explicitly called for scrutiny and independent measurements; this was an anomaly/hypothesis, **not established superluminal physics**. CERN's *same web page* later inserted February 2012 possible timing faults, March 2012 independent ICARUS confirmation of light-speed consistency and June 2012 corroboration from four experiments. A search result showing the page's original 2011 publication timestamp alongside the June 2012 update **must not expose the June result to a simulated September 2011 researcher**. February's possible oscillator or optical-fibre effects were **hypotheses pending testing** at that date, not yet certified diagnoses.

The analyzer extracts OPERA's improved 2012 neutrino arrival difference of **0.6 ± 0.4 statistical ± 3.0 systematic nanoseconds**, and independent ICARUS's **0.10 ± 0.67 statistical ± 2.39 systematic nanoseconds**. A conditional physical prediction combining the 2011 **20 ppm speed excess** with **730 kilometres** and defined speed of light yields **48.6994 nanoseconds early arrival** at that fixed path, versus 2012 measurements near zero. The *conditional prediction*, separately published observations and independent instrument corrections together **strongly disfavor** the original simple speed-excess hypothesis for these conditions. This is a reproducible derivation from public sources and known physical arithmetic, **not newly discovered physics**, not proof of an absolute speed limit without assumptions and not an estimator of the exact original systematic fault by itself. The report preserves statistical versus systematic errors separately; they may be correlated across laboratories and must not be added as if guaranteed independent.

Six independent deterministic audit functions in `test_primary_scientific_evidence.py` pass on the actual saved source pages and external labeled corpus. They verify that the wrong old actor remains preserved, the versioned corrected actor matches the original article metadata, the September 2011 evidence view excludes later 2012 results, the 2011 quoted conditional cannot become a measurement, and the two independent 2012 measurements/published scientific claim labels stay linked to their raw sources. These tests cover these **specific source versions and metadata procedures**, not general automatic actor resolution or a general discourse semantic parser.

The source-vintage and scope controls explicitly distinguish original measured observation, subsequent dated revision, separate laboratory/author and new hardware, quoted scientist opinion, conditional “if confirmed,” historical report versus current scientific consensus, neutrino versus antineutrino identity, and real experimental claim versus a hypothetical/dream wrapper. These are **reasoned counterfactual integrity requirements** for parsing; their manually specified hypothetical scope interventions are not independently annotated natural-language gold or a passed arbitrary-world DRS parser. The executable analyzer verifies actual publication-stage source links and arithmetic; future DRS testing must automatically extract each scope in the same input, under withheld gold.

## What approaches actually work, what is still conjecture

1. **Factual ingestion/attribution:** immutable original source, exact spans, URL/author/institution/version and availability date, quotation/fiction/dream/conditional scope, extracted value/unit/uncertainty and independent data producer identity. PROV-O/source tables represent derivation and responsibility; they do not certify truth. Compare source-aware retrieval + neighboring text, not bare lexical match.
2. **Finding candidate connections:** BM25/vector retrieval, entity and relation graphs, multi-document chain search, topic-dependent multi-view features and explicit *new hypothesis* tags. Swanson ABC literature bridging is evidence that new interesting possible links are obtainable, not that a proposed therapy works. Test alternative paths and disagreement.
3. **Valid formal conclusions:** first-order/Horn/typed logical rules, OWL open-world constraints, DRT/DRS for anaphora/negation and nested discourse worlds where it demonstrably improves over existing source scope, and SMT/SAT or independent symbolic evaluator for implications. Preserve *every* premise and its grounded source and uncertainty. The prior local narrow 1,024-case SAT agreement is a **mechanism feasibility result**, not empirical discovery.
4. **Scientific falsification and causality:** derive distinct testable predictions that discriminate H1 from H2, specify conditions and confounders, measure/intervene in a real environment, preregister where selection bias matters, handle noise and replicate independently. Invariance/causal design or sparse equation identification may outperform text-only reasoning in measured dynamical domains. Historical retrieval has a strict as-of-time barrier.
5. **Independent truth/novelty adjudication:** compare experiments, source family independence and publication prior art. Do not let many dependent abstracts, model seeds or sites count as separate factual replications. A result can be known elsewhere, ambiguous, conditionally contradicted or completely novel to a local index yet scientifically old. Calibrate extractor/model methods only after independent outcomes are resolved.

**DRT/DRS verdict:** formally appropriate for cross-paragraph anaphora, boxes, negation, conditionals, tense, dream and speech worlds; **no available successful same-corpus experiment demonstrates that KMD's model-extracted full DRS reliably beats a source-scoped parent/neighbor baseline in end-to-end scientific discovery**. TinyLlama's old ten-case 1/10 and malformed production-schema attempt provide only *small-model failure* evidence, not DRT's theoretical weakness. The scientific experiments here use lightweight source indexing, scoped metadata, independent human gold and physical measurement inference. If DRS is added, the exact improvement to measure is grounded factual conclusions, false-fact rate, cross-file identity, source-span recovery, attribution precision, anti-contradiction accuracy and experimental prediction quality at matched token/CPU time and gold, **not** the count of syntactically valid DRS boxes. The system's present three actor-identifier errors (`hrq086`, `hrq087`, `hrq089`), the additional context misses on newer revisions, and historical pytest aggregate failure/interruption remain negative evidence; no test gold or production parser was edited.

## Final scientific answer and limits

**Proven possible:** computer systems can generate candidate hypotheses not explicitly stated in one source and can make new mathematical or biological discoveries **when** proofs, independent instruments or biological experiments adjudicate them. Text retrieval can find scientific evidence but even a model shown the correct study often misjudges implication or polarity. Formal inference can establish logically new conditional consequences but cannot validate premises or establish empirical novelty without independent checks. The KMD project has source-linked storage and promising formal ingredients, but the central owner's claim—**reliably autonomous, genuinely novel, independently falsifiable scientific conclusions from arbitrary real documents**—has **not** been demonstrated for the deployed stack or these tests. Verified blockers are the lack of an isolated safe high-quality model slot for end-to-end Qwen DRS comparison, model extraction reliability, complex multi-document scope/identity, independent experimental ground truth for novel predictions, and fair uncontaminated evaluation. No new Calendar events, no shared GPU preemption and no changes to production KMD or Infra were made.

### Measured physical prediction vs distinct observations, with explicit assumptions

The additional source-based script `opera_conditional_prediction_20261024.py` reads the **unchanged corrected V2** extract, including original publisher and paper author, source dates and SHA-256, and emits an independent, non-overwriting `opera_conditional_prediction_results_20261024.json`. It models the *explicit* counterfactual hypothesis that the tentative September 2011 OPERA approximate **20 ppm speed excess** applies as the same constant neutrino propagation velocity throughout a **730-km** baseline and remains valid for the later measurement configurations. With the defined speed of light, this predicts **48.69938 ns** early arrival. The original CERN rounded historical figures are not identical to OPERA's exact original sixty-nanosecond anomaly, and this model is **not a derivation of that exact old experimental fit**.

Independently published later experimental source values are ICARUS 2012 **0.10 ± 0.67 (statistical) ± 2.39 (systematic) ns**, and OPERA 2012 **0.6 ± 0.4 ± 3.0 ns**, respectively. Under an *assumed* independent Gaussian quadrature model within each experiment, these have approximate combined errors **2.48 ns** and **3.03 ns**. The distance from the roughly 49-ns speed-excess prediction is then approximately **19.6** and **15.9** *assumed standard errors*, respectively. These are diagnostic effect sizes **conditional on the simplified constant-speed and error model**, **not formally calibrated discovery significance or a blanket probability that relativity is true**. In a deliberately hypothetical sensitivity analysis, adding a further independent **10-ns** unknown systematic error reduces the ICARUS standardized difference to about **4.7**, and a **30-ns** extra error reduces it to about **1.6**. Those extra errors are artificial robustness controls, **not measured instrument effects or an excuse to select errors after seeing outcomes**. Source identity, particle type, year and instrumental conditions are essential.

**Crucial distinction:** the different measurements are not *strict logical contradictions* if the measurements describe different apparatus, beams, times and systematic errors. Rather, taken as genuinely independent-source observations under declared overlap/comparability assumptions, they **discriminate against a single proposed time-invariant physical explanation** for the initial anomaly. March and June 2012 sources cannot be used in an as-of-September-2011 derivation; February's proposed clock/optical-fibre faults were not then verified effects; one CERN website publishing all its dated updates is not four independent publications. The report does not claim to have invented the conclusion; the novelty here is a reproducible KMD-style source-preserving extraction, identity-corrected and mathematically checked against independently published paper values. The original incorrect attribution and other KMD actor/identifier failures remain in the evidence ledger.

### Research-informed alternatives to discourse-representation-first reasoning

Additional primary source checked: Pearl and Bareinboim, *Transportability of Causal and Statistical Relations: A Formal Approach*, AAAI 2011, [DOI 10.1609/aaai.v25i1.7861](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v25i1.7861). Selection diagrams identify assumptions under which experimental results from one environment may validly be applied to another. The formal problem is **which measurements and conditions license transferring a causal claim**, rather than whether two documents' words are similar. Peters, Bühlmann and Meinshausen, *JRSS-B* 2016, [DOI 10.1111/rssb.12167](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12167), show how *invariance across environments* can identify causal variables under stated assumptions and test those assumptions with actual perturbation datasets. These approaches address precisely the failure of blindly merging 2011 and 2012 experiments as equivalent. They depend on the experimental design, not on an automatic DRS being semantically flawless.

The independently sourced paper *Mathematical discoveries from program search with large language models* by Romera-Paredes et al., *Nature* **print 2024, online 2023**, [DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6), makes a clear distinction from model voting: FunSearch accepts generated candidate programs only through its external evaluator, then can derive new mathematical constructions. The authors report gains and also disclose that some difficult outcomes occur in only a minority of runs; a single success is not a universal reliability claim. Robot Scientist Adam, King et al., *Science* 2009 [DOI 10.1126/science.1165620](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1165620), reports *automatically proposed hypotheses and actual lab tests with manual confirmation*. Later authors' evaluation [DOI 10.1186/1759-4499-2-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/1759-4499-2-1) also reports prior-known support and an incorrect conclusion caused by the biological system model omitting a relevant kinase mechanism. These are counterexamples to treating an internally consistent proof or a laboratory score as unconditional scientific truth.

**Conclusion:** for the actual original owner question, a useful agent must distinguish *syntactic/semantic extraction*, *conditional logical deduction*, *causal transportability and counterfactual predictions*, and *independently verified new empirical discovery*. Source-scoped neighboring retrieval, PROV-O-style provenance, solver-checkable constraints, and hypothesis-specific experimental tests are complementary techniques; DRT/DRS is only a candidate to improve long-distance referents and nested discourse scope. Nothing measured yet establishes production KMD model-extracted DRS as superior to the strongest source-aware neighbor and attribution baseline on real multi-document truth evaluation. Nor does the present work independently establish a genuinely novel scientific result. Further proof would require a blinded matched-budget DRS-vs-provenance trial on new expert-labeled multi-document examples, including author, quote, dream, hypothesis, identity, time and negation; and at least one externally adjudicated new prediction. The intended shared model instances remain unproven idle and were not disturbed.

### Genuine separated historical sources, actual negative evidence and hypothesis novelty limits

The automatic **as-of-December-1985** source subset consists of five independent peer-reviewed experimental studies and their source-scoped factual language:

- Tietjen et al., *Arch Surg*, November **1975**, [PMID 53042](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/53042/), [DOI 10.1001/archsurg.1975.01360170083011](https://doi.org/10.1001/archsurg.1975.01360170083011), measured greater viscosity in twenty people with Raynaud syndrome versus controls, but **sixteen of the twenty had scleroderma** and three other systemic inflammatory diseases. Only one had primary Raynaud disease. This is *mostly secondary disease evidence*, not primary Raynaud mechanism proof.
- Goyle and Dormandy, *Lancet*, June **1976**, [PMID 58309](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/58309/), [DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(76)92651-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(76)92651-9), observed higher viscosity in ten Raynaud patients than ten controls under **27°C and low-shear measurement**, explicitly suggesting a **possible** cause. The abstract does not reliably define patients' primary/secondary classifications. Neither a conditional hypothesis nor an association proves causal exclusivity.
- Jahnsen, Nielsen and Skovborg, *Lancet*, November **1977**, [PMID 72898](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/72898/), [DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(77)92895-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(77)92895-1), found **no statistically significant viscosity difference in ten women with primary Raynaud and ten matched controls**. More importantly, **venesection to lower plasma viscosity did not change the ease of provoking Raynaud's symptoms in five patients**. This is actual negative observational and small intervention evidence undermining a simple primary-Raynaud viscosity-only causal theory; it does not prove that viscosity has no role in every case.
- Blunt et al., *Br J Haematol*, August **1980**, [PMID 6775675](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6775675/), [DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2141.1980.tb07188.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2141.1980.tb07188.x), measured increased whole-blood viscosity in Raynaud and other vascular conditions. In the abstract, Raynaud subtype is not established, and comparison groups vary. This neither erases the 1977 null result nor proves a common causal mediator.
- Woodcock et al., *BMJ*, February **1984**, [PMID 6320945](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6320945/), [DOI 10.1136/bmj.288.6417.592](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.288.6417.592), independently randomized fish-oil/EPA-rich versus EPA-poor oil in **peripheral arterial disease**, finding a significant **whole-blood viscosity reduction at seven weeks**. It did **not** test primary Raynaud symptom improvement; treatment effect on one mediator in a different population cannot be silently transferred.

The source-aware automated lexical bridge found **one EPA/fish-oil–viscosity source** and **four Raynaud–viscosity sources** in this *preselected, curated* corpus. Combining their shared scientific intermediate term produced the candidate: “Could fish-oil supplementation affect Raynaud symptoms through a change in blood viscosity?” **This is a hypothesis-generating abductive association, not logical deduction**, because neither the mediator's causal necessity nor its transportability across patient populations was established. The corpus deliberately includes a real-world negative counterexample from 1977, which would be missed by a source-frequency-based agreement system. The shared token had **no direct fish-oil–Raynaud assertion in these five curated abstracts**; that does **not** prove no such claim existed anywhere before 1986.

Swanson's separately published original hypothesis paper, *Perspectives in Biology and Medicine* **1986** [PMID 3797213](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3797213/), [DOI 10.1353/pbm.1986.0087](https://doi.org/10.1353/pbm.1986.0087), **has no PubMed abstract**. The experiment therefore marks its 1986 full text **unavailable in the retrieved API**, rather than inventing an abstract and presenting a paper title as verified scientific reasoning. Swanson's own **1989** first-person letter in the *Journal of the American Society for Information Science*, preserved at [University of Pennsylvania's Garfield archive](https://garfield.library.upenn.edu/papers/jasis40%283%29p152y1989%281%29.html), explicitly retrospectively attributes the cross-literature hypothesis to his 1986 and 1987 publications and comments on the subsequent trial. That testimony helps establish the *author's own claim of novelty* but is **not an independent prior-art search, independent biological test, or information available to a researcher in 1985**. The source attribution checker separately identifies Don R. Swanson and the clinical investigators instead of counting their writings as multiple independent lab confirmations.

DiGiacomo, Kremer and Shah, *American Journal of Medicine*, February **1989**, [PMID 2536517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2536517/), [DOI 10.1016/0002-9343(89)90261-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-9343(89)90261-1), tested fish oil versus olive-oil placebo in **thirty-two randomized primary/secondary Raynaud patients**. Original abstract extraction recovered the reported timing change from **31.3 ± 1.3** minutes to **46.5 ± 2.1** minutes at six weeks (reported **p = 0.04**) in the fish-oil group: a descriptive **15.2-minute increase**. It also records the reported comparison of **five of eleven** primary patients versus **one of nine** olive-oil controls who no longer experienced inducible symptoms. The authors' conclusion was benefit for **primary but not established secondary Raynaud's**; there were no complete patient-level results for independent reanalysis of the published significance measures. A beneficial **clinical outcome** does **not verify that reduced whole-blood viscosity was the mechanism**. The earlier greater-viscosity evidence came predominantly from *secondary* disease, while an early null primary study and small failed viscosity-lowering intervention already challenged that proposed mediator. The subgroup inversion between source association and later response is an especially valuable causal **counterexample to a simplistic transitive knowledge-graph proof**, not proof that fish oil works by a particular alternative route.

### What was actually new, valid, tested or unproven in this reconstruction?

An as-of-1985 generator was capable of producing a **plausible cross-literature candidate** from two separately published mechanisms. It did **not** autonomously produce a biologically verified new claim, and the underlying Swanson hypothesis has been public since 1986, so a 2026 reconstruction is **not a novel discovery to the world**. The 1989 trial is independently authored and has a real experimental result, but the small, subgroup-dependent findings constitute bounded evidence rather than universal or modern clinical guidance. The studies differ in illness subtype, vascular condition, temperature, viscosity protocol, and timing; apparent disagreement is often **different scopes or uncertain heterogeneity**, not one same-referent/same-time logical contradiction.

One possible *prospective differentiating research question*, **not** an established new hypothesis or treatment recommendation, is: “If the clinical benefit operates mainly by reducing blood viscosity, does pre-treatment viscosity and the *measured mediator change* predict symptomatic response within separately randomized primary and secondary groups? If it does not, might vascular reactivity or another mechanism mediate the effect?” Testing that question requires actual mediator measurements, identifiable treatment-outcome and mediator-outcome relationships, control for post-treatment mediator–outcome confounding, exposure comparability and, ideally, independent replication. The classic treatment-to-mediator-to-outcome framework and sensitivity requirements are set out by Imai, Keele and Yamamoto, *Statistical Science* **2010**, [DOI 10.1214/10-STS321](https://doi.org/10.1214/10-STS321). **Randomizing fish oil does not automatically randomize mediator viscosity**: without additional assumptions or interventions, a later symptom improvement cannot adjudicate the specific viscosity-only explanation. The original clinical abstract did not supply the necessary causal mediator data.

### Independently established evaluation of general autonomous discovery

A relevant broader benchmark is Majumder et al., **DiscoveryBench**, *ICLR 2025*, [primary conference paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/0d70af566e69f1dfb687791ecf955e28-Abstract-Conference.html). It derived **264 real research workflow tasks** in six fields from published papers, with data, context, and known target insights; its evaluated strongest baseline achieved **25%** on the authors' discovery metric. These are *retrospectively constructed known-workflow tasks*, not 264 newly discovered facts, and those published numbers do not prove anything about the current installed KMD model. Jansen et al., **DiscoveryWorld**, *NeurIPS 2024*, [primary proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/13836f251823945316ae067350a5c366-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html), tests hypotheses, experimental actions, falsification and explanation across **twenty-four distinct simulated scientific tasks** with **120 parameterized challenges**; baseline agents struggled with most tasks. Simulation supplies controllable independent ground truth and intervention actions, but cannot substitute for real biological or physical measurement. These show why evidence-label accuracy, ordinary coding success, plausible answers and model agreement must not be called general scientific-discovery ability.

**Scientific bottom line:** The recorded user idea—source-grounded agent research that combines dispersed evidence into new testable questions—is scientifically sound and supported by Swanson's actual historical case and by experimental automation such as Robot Scientist Adam. **It is not equivalent to entailing causal treatment outcomes from co-occurring words, nor to formal proof of a medical mechanism**. The correct knowledge status is “candidate hypothesis” until a new experiment distinguishes explanations; as of 1985 the later trial is withheld; after 1989 evidence supports a limited outcome but still does not verify its proposed cause. A practical scope-rich provenance/neighbor retrieval system can preserve these distinctions without a DRS box. Whether full KMD model-extracted DRS improves over that baseline in new genuinely heldout real narratives is still unmeasured. The scripts and source-linked outputs keep this failure-to-establish mechanism visible, rather than silently converting a promising scientific association into a false fact.


## Cross-document SciFact expert evidence with scope-dependent correlations — October 24 continuation

The original owner explicitly warns against treating repeated assertions or mutually agreeing LLM outputs as truth. A new source-grounded **descriptive audit**, using the unchanged SciFact external 300-claim development split (already consumed in earlier analyses; **not** a new heldout test), examined every claim with **two or more independently indexed gold evidence documents**: `research/devtests_drt_reference/scientific_discovery_primary_20261024/scifact_multidoc_scope_audit.py`. Original corpus and development annotation hashes are fixed. Exactly **10 claims** had this property across **31 annotated paper records**. Seven claims had **SUPPORT-only** gold article annotations and three had **CONTRADICT-only** annotations. **Zero** had mutually opposite human source labels in this *limited particular subset*. That does **not** establish that science papers generally agree, that the annotated articles are statistically independent, or that all ten claims are empirically true/false. The script preserves original source document IDs, paper titles, exact annotation sentence indices, short excerpt hashes, label families and automatically detected qualifiers in `scifact_multidoc_scope_audit_results_20261024.json`.

Two notable real qualifier problems are **birth-weight/breast-cancer** claim IDs `179` and `1379`, for which several articles agree on a positive broad association while at least one original evidence sentence explicitly differentiates **premenopausal from postmenopausal** women. It is invalid to promote an age/menopause-restricted association to a universal effect. Claims `597` and `971` contain papers discussing **incidence and/or mortality** in cervical disease; incidence is not the same outcome as mortality, and evidence retrieval must not silently collapse those endpoints. These are **automatic keyword-qualifier observations attached to actual source sentence IDs**, not human-scored proof that KMD resolved the entity/scope correctly. In these records no pair of opposite gold labels proves a real cross-author contradiction, but subtype and outcome divergence still demand exact definitions.

Another problem is *correlated evidence*. Multiple birth-weight articles include systematic reviews, primary-study reanalyses and meta-analyses potentially drawing on overlapping original participants. Merely finding four concordant paper titles does not produce four independent experiments. The [Cochrane Handbook chapter on overviews of reviews](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-v) explicitly requires mapping included primary studies across reviews, using a citation matrix and, where useful, corrected covered area. [Hennessy and Johnson 2020, DOI 10.1002/jrsm.1390](https://doi.org/10.1002/jrsm.1390) analyze methods for overlap and non-independence. **No overlap statistic was inferred from abstracts alone** here; original citation matrices and primary cohorts were not supplied. A provenance graph would need to retain not just paper author/URL but which original trial or dataset each review or derivative conclusion cites. This is an important real scientific-knowledge requirement **not solved by DRS boxes, a similarity threshold, or a majority vote**.


## New source-aware Qwen model inference negative result — October 24 continuation

A local CPU-only published Qwen2.5-1.5B model was compared against its own attempted source-only DRS-inspired boxed extraction on eight newly frozen real SciFact paper pairs, balanced four externally annotated SUPPORT and four CONTRADICT; evidence text and paper title were supplied with human label *withheld*. **Direct judgments declared SUPPORT on all eight**, correctly agreeing in four support cases but **incorrectly agreeing in all four contradictions**. An after-inference independent rationale audit established that **each one of these four contradiction cases contained the original expert-evidence sentence inside the model's prompt**. The missed differences were substantial: numeric amount, categorical ability, opposite association direction and equal-versus-slower rate. A source copied into the response therefore did **not guarantee semantic understanding or factual validity**. The full raw output and original source hashes are in `research/devtests_drt_reference/qwen_scifact_20261024/`.

A more structured *model-extracted* route attempted compact DRS-inspired subordinate boxes and grounded conditions, but all eight JSON generations reached their fixed 195-token budgets and failed validity checks. No second model verdict was generated: all UNKNOWN results were **safe rejection, not a functioning boxed reasoner**. This real-source negative result must not be promoted to evidence that full formal DRT is inherently inferior to provenance-neighbor retrieval: the tested small model and token budgets, source selection and extraction schema differ from production KMD. The comparison used identical source material but *unequal output complexity* and no completed multi-document inference. The specialized published neural PMB parser's scope-operator strengths are reported in `kmd_discourse_and_scientific_reasoning_2026-10-24.md`, where its gold test is short utterances rather than research papers.

The separate SciFact descriptive cross-document audit on this already-consumed development split identified ten claims with 31 annotated papers, seven consistently SUPPORT and three consistently CONTRADICT, with **no paper-to-paper opposite labels** among those ten. This does not constitute 31 independent experiments or establish underlying truth: systematic reviews can reuse the same study population and labels collapse source population distinctions.
