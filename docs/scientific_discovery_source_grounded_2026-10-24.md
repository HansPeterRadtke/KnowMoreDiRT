# Can KnowMoreDiRT support genuine scientific discovery? Corrective research, 2026-10-24

**Direct research answer.** Yes, computer systems have independently demonstrated genuine hypothesis generation, deductively valid mathematics and experimentally tested novel scientific conclusions, but their successful examples rely on something outside an LLM's opinion: an executable evaluator, independently reproduced laboratory observations or a carefully defined causal/experimental design. No published scientific result in the sources below proves that the current KnowMoreDiRT (KMD) system reliably generates *true, new discoveries* from arbitrary documents. The strongest supported option for the actual requested goal is a **source-preserving, scope-aware retrieval and evidence pipeline plus formal verification where premises support it and independently discriminating experiments for empirical hypotheses**. DRT/DRS is *one possible interpretation and long-distance reference layer*, not proof of truth and not a predetermined winner over a provenance-plus-neighbor baseline.

## Original research requirement, not an invented how-to question

Relevant complete normalized primary guidance is `knowledge_retrieval.md`, `kmd/knowledge_and_memory_design.md`, `agents/research_and_evidence.md`, and the research appendix, all under Infra guidelines. References here are original source-relative identifiers, **not accessible relative links from this separate repository**. The recordings' question is not simply whether an agent can summarize and agree with itself. It is whether a source-grounded research agent can distinguish genuinely unknown claims from negations, form new useful hypotheses, derive conditional consequences, seek contradictions, and have later independent outcomes decisively discriminate between them. Sources from separate authors, contexts and dates may disagree without logically contradicting one another; all statements must retain speaker, scope, time and epistemic status. The owner explicitly treats model ensemble votes as additional observations, never proof.

The proper evaluation outcome has several mutually exclusive evidence statuses:

- **Retrieved statement:** a quotation or document says a proposition; the system has not endorsed it as true.
- **Interpreted proposition:** a fallible extraction identifies referent, predicate, negation, attribution, time and conditional/dream/quotation scope; errors here contaminate all later proofs.
- **Derivation:** a rule yields a conclusion valid *if* its exact premises and scope are correct; a formal prover may check this without making the premises factual.
- **Contradiction:** scoped premises assert logically incompatible propositions in the same identity/conditions/time. This is evidence of **source disagreement or internal inconsistency**, not a decision about which source is true.
- **Research hypothesis:** an unobserved candidate explanation, mechanistic link or novel prediction potentially worth testing, often from non-obvious cross-paper connections.
- **Empirically tested outcome:** a measured intervention or discriminating observation bears on that prediction under declared assumptions; limitations, confounding, calibration and replication remain.
- **Scientifically established novelty:** independently adjudicated prior-art search and independent evaluation show the prediction or result adds something not previously known. “Novel to our database” is insufficient.

## Scientific primary evidence that directly answers the goal

**Robot Scientist Adam—novel biological conclusions and independent experimental checks.** King, Rowland, Oliver et al., *Science* 2009, “The automation of science” (doi: [10.1126/science.1165620](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1165620)), report an agent that constructed hypotheses about unknown yeast enzyme/gene functions and experimentally tested them through laboratory automation. The authors' follow-on detailed evaluation “Towards Robot Scientists for autonomous scientific discovery,” *Automated Experimentation* 2010 ([doi:10.1186/1759-4499-2-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/1759-4499-2-1)), says twenty hypotheses were proposed, twelve had strong robotic experimental confirmation, **three were additionally verified by conventional manual biological experiments** and **six more had support already discoverable by detailed literature searches**. A separate first-person retrospective in *Scientific American* reports seven conclusions as already known, one wrong and twelve novel candidates, illustrating a different denominator and classification; do **not** pool those numbers uncritically. This is exceptionally relevant negative evidence: a robot can generate valid, sometimes novel insight, but it can also call something novel when the literature already knew it, and it can make a wrong conclusion. Primary sources: [King 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19342587/), [2010 project follow-up](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1759-4499-2-1), and [authors' retrospective](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/rise-of-the-robo-scientists/).

**FunSearch—new formally checkable mathematical results, not an LLM vote.** Romera-Paredes et al., *Nature* 2024, [doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6). A language model generated executable programs, an external deterministic evaluator rated results, and evolutionary search retained candidates that improved on established constructions for cap sets and bin-packing heuristics. Its strongest claim is grounded by numerical/mathematical verification of a generated program. It does not show that a text-only agent may endorse new physical facts without measurements. General mechanism: **generative conjecture + independent objective test + counterexamples + preserved search provenance**.

**Coscientist—LLMs can link instructions, literature, instruments and experiments, with autonomy limits.** Boiko, MacKnight, Kline and Gomes, *Nature* 2023, [doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06792-0](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06792-0). Published tests include documentation-driven instrument operation, synthesis planning and palladium-catalysed cross-coupling reaction optimization with laboratory automation; the authors explicitly describe *(semi-)autonomous* experimental execution. This is evidence of useful workflow orchestration and physical feedback, not of universally reliable original mechanistic theories or removal of human laboratory boundaries.

**Literature-based discovery: Swanson's fish oil–Raynaud's connection.** D. R. Swanson, “Fish oil, Raynaud's syndrome, and undiscovered public knowledge,” *Perspectives in Biology and Medicine* 1986, [PMID 3797213](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3797213/). He joined disjoint research on fish oil/blood properties and Raynaud's vascular effects into a **hypothesis**, not a valid deductive certainty that fish oil treats the syndrome. The causal link requires biological assumptions and actual clinical verification. A subsequent independent randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial by DiGiacomo, Kremer and Shah, *American Journal of Medicine* 1989, [PMID 2536517](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2536517/), randomized thirty-two primary/secondary Raynaud's patients. It found some positive measures for **primary** Raynaud's and no established improvement for **secondary** Raynaud's. It does **not** prove the original broad therapeutic hypothesis universally, and small samples require replication. This is a precise example of **literature-guided genuinely new conjecture followed by experimental constraints and subgroup-dependent conclusions**, without conflating corroboration with universal truth.

**Automatic equation/model discovery and causal invariance have different requirements.** Brunton, Proctor and Kutz, “Discovering governing equations from data by sparse identification of nonlinear dynamical systems,” *PNAS* 2016, [doi:10.1073/pnas.1517384113](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1517384113): sparse regression over observed dynamics can recover useful equations **given measured state variables, derivative estimation and candidate function libraries**; simulations and external predictive validation matter. Peters, Bühlmann and Meinshausen, “Causal inference by using invariant prediction,” *JRSS B* 2016, [primary manuscript](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~peterbu/Files/Manuscripts/invariant-causal-prediction.pdf): observational correlation alone is not a causal intervention; candidates remaining invariant across changed environments can support identification under stated assumptions. These methods are better for physics/causal claims when time series or interventions exist than purely textual DRS or model agreement.

**Document grounding and logical representations solve other, earlier failure modes.** Wadden et al., *EMNLP* 2020, [SciFact](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/), supplies published expert claim-to-paper evidence/rationale labels rather than assistant agreement. Jiang et al., *EMNLP Findings* 2020, [HoVer](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.309/), demonstrates that requiring support across two to four separate documents degrades semantic-matching accuracy. W3C [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) represents attributed entities, generated derivations, quotations, revisions and involved agents; it does **not** establish that a quoted claim is true. Formal DRT (Kamp, van Genabith and Reyle 2011; exact primary PDF in existing `docs/papers/`), OWL 2 [primer](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-primer/) and solver logic [SMT-LIB](https://smt-lib.org/) each provide different mechanisms: natural-language referents and subordinate scope; declarative ontologies with open-world interpretations; and satisfiability/entailment under supplied predicates, respectively. Probabilistic weights or source frequency do not automatically transform observation into truth. Source-dependent premises require source-dependent proof trees and trusted measurement protocols.

## Local new experimental evidence: real scientific documents, not artificial cases

### Full SciFact retrieval and actual automatic rationale search

External authoritative input acquired as the exact [allenai/SciFact release](https://github.com/allenai/scifact) from archive `https://scifact.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/release/latest/data.tar.gz`, downloaded into research-only `/data/tmp/kmd-scifact-release-20261024/data`. Upstream code commit `68b98a56d93e0f9da0d2aab4e6c3294699a0f72e`; original corpus SHA-256 `b8d6c89624cb2ed74dee8938effc4f5d8bd2086887880af8110d64be4ceade62`, claims development SHA-256 `86f0435d08fdb65d1aa41d1472684f57e6e71930626497bdf4d7a9ec1a632217`, claims training SHA-256 `f4c8fa82d8bd0653a9cc8d61a6ea48c25eacea64e90af5dbf390ebb1b74372f0`. Original dataset claims/evidence use **CC BY 4.0**; S2ORC article abstracts **ODC-By 1.0**; authors and license recorded here for attribution. The exact **unmodified compressed SciFact original dataset** and upstream dataset-license file are also preserved in this owning research directory for stable reproduction, with source attribution as above. Original text was never altered; dataset validation is enforced by hashes. Full CERN/arXiv HTML remains at read-only research-local temporary paths and is traceable via exact source URLs, file hashes and preserved excerpts rather than redistributed as copyrighted HTML. Published data have third-party expert evidence annotation, but annotations are evidence-level labels, **not independent physical replication** and their existence does not mean every scientific proposition is ultimately true.

`research/devtests_drt_reference/scientific_discovery_primary_20261024/scifact_retrieval_ablation.py` processes **all 5,183 abstracts** against **300 development claims**; **188** have at least one expert-labeled supporting/contradicting abstract and **112** have no such annotated evidence, which does **not** imply those claims are false. Ten have multiple labeled evidence documents. No labels, evidence IDs or source statuses enter candidate rankings. Title TF-IDF, title+abstract TF-IDF and full-document BM25 are frozen untuned source-preserving baselines. For the 188 claims, the number with **at least one labeled document among the first ten** is, respectively, **126**, **171**, **178**; BM25 first-ranked document contains labeled evidence for **135** of the 188, compared with **71** title-only and **121** full TF-IDF. BM25 returned **all** the gold evidence docs within the first ten for **172/188**. Bounded CPU work finished in approximately twelve seconds, with exact full ranked IDs and source hashes preserved in `scifact_fixed_retrieval_results_20261024.json`.

With **oracle knowledge of the correct evidence-containing document** but *automatic* sentence selection (fixed token-overlap top two), at least one annotated rationale sentence was included in **139/209** claim-document pairs; neighboring sentence expansion raised that to **161/209** with mean context about 4.6 sentences. This is a meaningful measured benefit of source-neighbor expansion. It does not demonstrate discourse interpretation, agreement among models or end-to-end claim verification, and **no KMD DRS result was measured** on the same corpus. The development split has now been consumed and must not be retuned and re-labeled as a fresh holdout.

### Scientific NLI interpretation fails despite the correct article being known

`scifact_distinct_train_nli.py` used a **separate, non-overlapping sample from SciFact training annotations**, selected by the SHA-256 order of claim/document identifiers *before* inference, with **96 distinct third-party expert-labeled article–claim pairs**, not the previous development claims. Every case deliberately provides the **correct evidence document** but selects the **top two sentences automatically** by lexical overlap. A downloaded, off-the-shelf generic `cross-encoder/nli-MiniLM2-L6-H768` ONNX quantized model (revision `b95119ce93d3e065de6214e38cd4a97b0f2f2c6d`) assigns `SUPPORT`, `CONTRADICT` or `NEUTRAL`; no task fine-tuning, no dev-label threshold optimization, zero shared GPU, approximately eighteen seconds CPU for 192 sentence pairs. Fixed question/document selection, original label evidence, model probabilities, sentence IDs and source identity are in `scifact_distinct_train_nli_frozen_cases_20261024.json` and `scifact_distinct_train_nli_results_20261024.json`.

**Only 39/96** model conclusions matched expert labels: nineteen correct of fifty-nine annotated SUPPORT cases, twenty correct of thirty-seven annotated CONTRADICT cases; forty-three returned NEUTRAL despite labeled evidence, and **fourteen asserted the opposite** of the gold (three contradictory claims mislabeled as support; eleven supported claims mislabeled contradiction). A post-hoc always-SUPPORT classifier would match 59/96 because this particular evidence-conditioned sample contains 59 supported claims; this descriptive comparison was **not** a predeclared tuned benchmark. One concrete wrong-polarity case: claim ID 1288 states statin use positively correlates with hip fractures, but the paper “HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors and the risk of hip fractures in elderly patients” reports statin-associated **reduced** fracture risk. The model marked SUPPORT although expert evidence marked CONTRADICT. Another opposite-polarity case: claim ID 426 on FoxO transcription factors participating in apoptosis; the article expressly identifies apoptosis among FoxO processes yet the model marked CONTRADICT against the expert SUPPORT label. These real automatically extracted **wrong conclusions** falsify any assumption that correct source retrieval alone suffices for trustworthy scientific conclusions. Exact article and claim evidence appear in the **external original SciFact corpus** (not invented in this report).

An earlier **distinct development** ONNX run completed generation on 748 actual sentence pairs but then failed during result aggregation (`KeyError: 'oracle_evidence_document'` when a claim had no annotated gold document). Because its probabilities were not persisted and execution cannot be independently checked afterward, **no dev-model accuracy is reported**. It was not silently replayed or converted into a fictitious benchmark. A separate small TinyLlama test from prior research had invalid JSON outputs; these and the historical KMD actor-role failures remain preserved. Even the newer ninety-six-case score assesses textual expert-label consistency, **not autonomous independent experiments, causal proof, DRS parsing or actual new scientific hypotheses**.

### Cross-publisher and historical claim scope: OPERA vs ICARUS

The scientific case was constructed from preserved primary archived source HTML, **not** hand-invented measurement numbers. The initial `real_neutrino_source_reasoning.py` automatically reads (a) the [historical CERN OPERA statement with its embedded dated 2012 updates](https://home.cern/opera-experiment-reports-anomaly-in-flight-time-of-neutrinos-from-cern-to-gran-sasso/), (b) OPERA's later timing instrumentation paper [arXiv:1212.1276](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1276) and (c) independent ICARUS's [arXiv:1208.2629](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.2629). **A real extraction failure was discovered:** arXiv's generic first page heading (“High Energy Physics - Experiment”) was mistakenly read as its paper title, and the older `real_neutrino_source_analysis_results_20261024.json` therefore mislabeled an **OPERA** measurement as **ICARUS**. The original failed output remains **unaltered** with a recorded SHA-256. The separately versioned `real_neutrino_source_reasoning_corrected.py` now reads the source's actual `citation_title`, `citation_author` and `citation_date` metadata, independently checks that paper/source identity matches, and produces `real_neutrino_source_analysis_corrected_20261024.json`. The corrected output identifies OPERA Collaboration, paper dated December 6, 2012, and ICARUS (Antonello et al.), paper dated August 13, 2012, as **different producer groups**; measured values remained unchanged. Exact source HTML SHA-256 values, source paragraph hashes, publisher, effective date, extracted numeric strings and original article metadata are retained. The original HTML is preserved read-only in `/data/tmp/kmd-scientific-realworld-sources-20261024`, not copied into Git. This negative result establishes that even an automatically correct numeric extraction can be scientifically invalid if the experimental actor is bound incorrectly. Do not silently relabel a previously failed result as a pass.

CERN's 23 September 2011 original release reported an **apparent** speed excess of approximately **twenty parts per million** over a **730-kilometre** neutrino flight. It explicitly called for scrutiny and independent measurements; this was an anomaly/hypothesis, **not established superluminal physics**. CERN's *same web page* later inserted February 2012 possible timing faults, March 2012 independent ICARUS confirmation of light-speed consistency and June 2012 corroboration from four experiments. A search result showing the page's original 2011 publication timestamp alongside the June 2012 update **must not expose the June result to a simulated September 2011 researcher**. February's possible oscillator or optical-fibre effects were **hypotheses pending testing** at that date, not yet certified diagnoses.

The analyzer extracts OPERA's improved 2012 neutrino arrival difference of **0.6 ± 0.4 statistical ± 3.0 systematic nanoseconds**, and independent ICARUS's **0.10 ± 0.67 statistical ± 2.39 systematic nanoseconds**. A conditional physical prediction combining the 2011 **20 ppm speed excess** with **730 kilometres** and defined speed of light yields **48.6994 nanoseconds early arrival** at that fixed path, versus 2012 measurements near zero. The *conditional prediction*, separately published observations and independent instrument corrections together **strongly disfavor** the original simple speed-excess hypothesis for these conditions. This is a reproducible derivation from public sources and known physical arithmetic, **not newly discovered physics**, not proof of an absolute speed limit without assumptions and not an estimator of the exact original systematic fault by itself. The report preserves statistical versus systematic errors separately; they may be correlated across laboratories and must not be added as if guaranteed independent.

Six independent deterministic audit functions in `test_primary_scientific_evidence.py` pass on the actual saved source pages and external labeled corpus. They verify that the wrong old actor remains preserved, the versioned corrected actor matches the original article metadata, the September 2011 evidence view excludes later 2012 results, the 2011 quoted conditional cannot become a measurement, and the two independent 2012 measurements/published scientific claim labels stay linked to their raw sources. These tests cover these **specific source versions and metadata procedures**, not general automatic actor resolution or a general discourse semantic parser.

The source-vintage and scope controls explicitly distinguish original measured observation, subsequent dated revision, separate laboratory/author and new hardware, quoted scientist opinion, conditional “if confirmed,” historical report versus current scientific consensus, neutrino versus antineutrino identity, and real experimental claim versus a hypothetical/dream wrapper. These are **reasoned counterfactual integrity requirements** for parsing; their manually specified hypothetical scope interventions are not independently annotated natural-language gold or a passed arbitrary-world DRS parser. The executable analyzer verifies actual publication-stage source links and arithmetic; future DRS testing must automatically extract each scope in the same input, under withheld gold.

## What approaches actually work, what is still conjecture

1. **Factual ingestion/attribution:** immutable original source, exact spans, URL/author/institution/version and availability date, quotation/fiction/dream/conditional scope, extracted value/unit/uncertainty and independent data producer identity. PROV-O/source tables represent derivation and responsibility; they do not certify truth. Compare source-aware retrieval + neighboring text, not bare lexical match.
2. **Finding candidate connections:** BM25/vector retrieval, entity and relation graphs, multi-document chain search, topic-dependent multi-view features and explicit *new hypothesis* tags. Swanson ABC literature bridging is evidence that new interesting possible links are obtainable, not that a proposed therapy works. Test alternative paths and disagreement.
3. **Valid formal conclusions:** first-order/Horn/typed logical rules, OWL open-world constraints, DRT/DRS for anaphora/negation and nested discourse worlds where it demonstrably improves over existing source scope, and SMT/SAT or independent symbolic evaluator for implications. Preserve *every* premise and its grounded source and uncertainty. The prior local narrow 1,024-case SAT agreement is a **mechanism feasibility result**, not empirical discovery.
4. **Scientific falsification and causality:** derive distinct testable predictions that discriminate H1 from H2, specify conditions and confounders, measure/intervene in a real environment, preregister where selection bias matters, handle noise and replicate independently. Invariance/causal design or sparse equation identification may outperform text-only reasoning in measured dynamical domains. Historical retrieval has a strict as-of-time barrier.
5. **Independent truth/novelty adjudication:** compare experiments, source family independence and publication prior art. Do not let many dependent abstracts, model seeds or sites count as separate factual replications. A result can be known elsewhere, ambiguous, conditionally contradicted or completely novel to a local index yet scientifically old. Calibrate extractor/model methods only after independent outcomes are resolved.

**DRT/DRS verdict:** formally appropriate for cross-paragraph anaphora, boxes, negation, conditionals, tense, dream and speech worlds; **no available successful same-corpus experiment demonstrates that KMD's model-extracted full DRS reliably beats a source-scoped parent/neighbor baseline in end-to-end scientific discovery**. TinyLlama's old ten-case 1/10 and malformed production-schema attempt provide only *small-model failure* evidence, not DRT's theoretical weakness. The scientific experiments here use lightweight source indexing, scoped metadata, independent human gold and physical measurement inference. If DRS is added, the exact improvement to measure is grounded factual conclusions, false-fact rate, cross-file identity, source-span recovery, attribution precision, anti-contradiction accuracy and experimental prediction quality at matched token/CPU time and gold, **not** the count of syntactically valid DRS boxes. The system's present three actor-identifier errors (`hrq086`, `hrq087`, `hrq089`), the additional context misses on newer revisions, and historical pytest aggregate failure/interruption remain negative evidence; no test gold or production parser was edited.

## Final scientific answer and limits

**Proven possible:** computer systems can generate candidate hypotheses not explicitly stated in one source and can make new mathematical or biological discoveries **when** proofs, independent instruments or biological experiments adjudicate them. Text retrieval can find scientific evidence but even a model shown the correct study often misjudges implication or polarity. Formal inference can establish logically new conditional consequences but cannot validate premises or establish empirical novelty without independent checks. The KMD project has source-linked storage and promising formal ingredients, but the central owner's claim—**reliably autonomous, genuinely novel, independently falsifiable scientific conclusions from arbitrary real documents**—has **not** been demonstrated for the deployed stack or these tests. Verified blockers are the lack of an isolated safe high-quality model slot for end-to-end Qwen DRS comparison, model extraction reliability, complex multi-document scope/identity, independent experimental ground truth for novel predictions, and fair uncontaminated evaluation. No new Calendar events, no shared GPU preemption and no changes to production KMD or Infra were made.
