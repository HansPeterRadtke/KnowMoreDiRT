# Real scientific claims: isolated Qwen vs lightweight boxed semantics (2026-10-24)

**This experiment is a genuine model-generated semantic representation test on previously unused REAL SciFact source documents, not a toy proof or a manually supplied perfect DRS. It is also NOT production KMD `chunk-drs-v5`, not true long-range discourse research and not a blinded science discovery benchmark.**

Original third-party expert-labeled source: [SciFact, Wadden et al., EMNLP 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/), [original AllenAI dataset](https://github.com/allenai/scifact), [original corpus and claim releases](https://scifact.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/release/latest/data.tar.gz), original text/evidence license details in adjacent `../scientific_discovery_primary_20261024/SCIFACT_SOURCE_LICENSE.md`. The original immutable source bytes remain at `/data/tmp/kmd-scifact-release-20261024/data`. The model is official `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF`, pinned HuggingFace revision `91cad51170dc346986eccefdc2dd33a9da36ead9`, `qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf` at `/data/tmp/kmd-qwen-small-isolated-20261024/`, size 1,117,320,736 bytes and SHA-256 `6a1a2eb6d15622bf3c96857206351ba97e1af16c30d7a74ee38970e434e9407e`; Apache 2.0. No GPU or shared inference service was used. Raspi CPU model process was lowered to nice 15, two inference threads, model context 1,900, temperature zero and seed 42. No other running KMD/agent process was preempted.

`freeze_preexisting_labelled_science.py` fixed the **eight** new source pairs from the original published SciFact *training* split, explicitly excluding all 96 pairs in the prior October 24 ONNX scientific experiment. Four human-annotated SUPPORT and four CONTRADICT were selected using hash sorting within each externally annotated class (stratification is a selection choice, not a model prompt). **Human gold labels and gold evidence sentence indices were not supplied to Qwen.** The same fixed lexical query-to-abstract sentence search selects two sentences with one neighbor either side; the source title and selected actual text are supplied unchanged to both methods. `frozen_model_science_20261024.json` is immutable input and records source IDs, hashes and complete selected source. It is *locally* held out from prior research usage but may be present in the pretrained model's original training data; the word “heldout” does not warrant unseen-to-pretraining.

`run_isolated_science_box_ablation.py` executed exactly once with checkpointing: (A) direct source-aware claim verdict plus copied evidence phrase, and (B) a source-only model-generated **DRS-inspired compact structure** with explicit boxes, scoped proposition conditions and exact-source evidence strings, intended to support a later *separate* claim judgment. The fixed parse output budget was **195 tokens** and original evidence spans had 660–1,348 characters. All eight compact semantic extractions reached `finish_reason='length'`, were incomplete JSON, and therefore failed the grounding-and-structure validator. **The second claim judgment in method B was never invoked.** It emitted UNKNOWN by explicit safe abstention; do not misrepresent the 0/8 pipeline score as an observed accuracy of a working DRS reasoner. Larger budgets, different models and full KMD schema could change this. A correct refusal is preferable to accepting an incomplete pseudo-fact representation.

`score_frozen_science.py` scored the eight *completed raw checkpoint records* against original expert labels after all inference had ended and wrote `labelled_model_comparison_20261024.json`; no gold or model outputs were changed. Direct model returned SUPPORT **eight out of eight times**, for **4/8** exact expert-label agreement because exactly four were human-annotated CONTRADICT. Seven direct evidence quotes were verbatim substrings from the source, but a verbatim quotation does **not mean the model interpreted its polarity correctly**. `audit_labelled_source_coverage.py` then independently checked original expert rationale sentence IDs in the actual selected input: **7/8** include at least one gold rationale sentence, including **all four** CONTRADICT cases. In those four, the model quoted the very original passage but still claimed SUPPORT. These are real demonstrable grounded semantic failures, not missing retrieval or a model judging from no source. One positively scored answer lacks an annotated rationale in its input; that score could reflect plausible guessing or another supporting sentence rather than correct evidence interpretation. See `source_coverage_and_extraction_failure_audit_20261024.json`.

Concrete negative examples with unchanged source IDs and gold sentence offsets: `sci-178-23557241` asks whether higher birth-weight is *negatively* associated with breast cancer, while its retrieved meta-analysis reports *higher birth-weight increases risk*; `sci-1184-16737210` says a kidney donation policy saves *$5 billion*, while source evidence states *up to $750 million*; `sci-925-17077004` asserts *equal* HIV progression rates for partnered/unpartnered patients, while the paper reports *slower* rates with stable partnership; `sci-772-24922825` says mice *cannot* produce neutralizing CHIKV antibodies, while the cited vaccine study reports that neutralizing antibodies were elicited in mice. The model called all four SUPPORT. These illustrate sign reversal, numeric magnitude, equality versus reduction, and categorical negation errors. **All source evidence and human gold were preserved without repairs.**

Resource cost: direct decisions consumed approximately 189.8 seconds total; source-only boxed extraction approximately 380.6 seconds, before any potential second reasoning call. These are *unmatched* budgets and a single tiny model; they are not a valid causal attribution of quality to adding DRS. The definitive unresolved research is a matched-budget, larger-model *production KMD* DRS parsing and executable question-answering evaluation against a provenance/neighbor baseline on genuinely independent **multi-document** text including attributed quotations, dreams, negations, changing actor identity and time. The original three KMD hard actor identifier failures (`hrq086`, `hrq087`, `hrq089`), later two contextual hard cases and historical full-suite issues are not modified by this experiment.

Preserved artifacts: `frozen_model_science_20261024.json`, `results_checkpoint.jsonl` (complete verbatim model outputs/token counts), `labelled_model_comparison_20261024.json` (initial direct-vs-abstain score, its `invalid_boxed=0` refers only to the syntactically valid fallback UNKNOWN verdict, **not** zero invalid extracted frames), and `source_coverage_and_extraction_failure_audit_20261024.json` (explicit eight rejected/truncated boxes and original human rationale coverage). Re-run scripts deliberately refuse to overwrite original outcomes.
